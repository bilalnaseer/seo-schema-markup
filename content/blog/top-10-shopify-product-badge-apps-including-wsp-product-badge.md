Choosing the right Shopify product badge app can make a noticeable difference in how customers interact with your online store. Product badges help highlight discounts, new arrivals, bestsellers, limited stock, and special offers without requiring customers to read lengthy product descriptions.

Whether you want to increase product visibility, promote seasonal offers, or make important product details stand out, the right app can simplify the process.

In this guide, we explore the **Top 10 Shopify Product Badge Apps**, including free and paid options, their key features, and what makes each one useful for different Shopify stores.

## **Top 10 Shopify Product Badge Apps**

Here are ten Shopify apps worth considering when looking for product labels, promotional stickers, and customizable badges.

### **1. WSP – Product Badge**

**Best for: Automated product badges and easy customization**

[WSP – Product Badge](https://apps.shopify.com/websensepro-product-badge) is our top recommendation for Shopify merchants who want an easy way to highlight important product information.

Developed by WebSensePro, this app helps store owners display attractive badges without editing theme code. It supports automatic badges for different product conditions, making product promotion easier to manage.

**Key Features:**

- Automatic Sale, New Arrival, Low Stock, Sold Out, and Top Seller badges.
- Custom product badge creation.
- Flexible badge positioning and styling.
- Scheduled badges with specific start and end dates.
- Automatic updates when product prices, inventory, or collections change.
- Display badges on product, collection, search, and homepage pages.

Another advantage is its theme app embed functionality, which eliminates the need for manual theme code modifications.

For merchants looking for a straightforward product badge solution, WSP – Product Badge is an excellent starting point.

### **2. DECO Product Labels & Badges**

DECO helps Shopify merchants create promotional labels and badges for their product catalogs. It offers options for highlighting discounts, seasonal campaigns, and special offers.

### **3. Sami AI Product Labels & Badge**

**Best for: AI-powered product badges**

Sami provides product labels, promotional stickers, and trust badges. Merchants can use AI-generated designs and targeting conditions to create different promotional campaigns.

### **4. Product Labels & Badges – Lably**

Lably offers ready-made templates and customization options for product labels. Merchants can adjust colors, fonts, positions, and display conditions to match their storefront design.

### **5. Hoppy Trust Badges & Icons**

Hoppy focuses on trust badges, payment icons, and social proof elements. It can help communicate important purchasing information and reinforce customer confidence.

### **6. Essential Trust Badges & Icons**

This app offers trust badges, feature icons, labels, and banners. It is useful for stores that want to communicate shipping benefits, guarantees, and other purchasing assurances.

### **7. TA AI Product Labels & Badges**

**Best for: AI-assisted promotional labels**

TA AI helps merchants highlight products through promotional badges, stickers, and customizable labels. It is another option for stores running frequent marketing campaigns.

### **8. Rimix Product Badges & Labels**

Rimix provides options for displaying Sale, New Arrival, and other product badges. It is worth exploring for merchants who need straightforward product highlighting.

### **9. ShineTrust – Product Badges**

**Best for: Product promotion and visual customization**

ShineTrust is another option for merchants interested in product labels and promotional badges. It can help make important product offers more noticeable across a storefront.

### **10. Flair**

## **Why Should Shopify Stores Use Product Badge Apps?**

Product badges provide quick visual information that helps shoppers understand product offers without searching through descriptions.

Some common benefits include:

- **Better product visibility:** Highlight important products in collection pages.
- **Promotional awareness:** Make discounts and seasonal offers easier to notice.
- **Improved shopping experience:** Communicate product availability and special features.
- **Campaign management:** Display relevant badges during promotional periods.
- **Product discovery:** Help customers identify new arrivals and popular products.

## **How to Choose the Best Shopify Product Badge App**

Before installing a Shopify product badge app, consider these factors:

1. **Customization:** Look for different colors, shapes, positions, and text options.
2. **Automation:** Choose apps that automatically update badges based on product conditions.
3. **Theme compatibility:** Make sure the app works with your current Shopify theme.
4. **Pricing:** Compare free features and paid plans before making a decision.
5. **Performance:** Avoid unnecessary features that could affect your storefront experience.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

### **What is the best Shopify product badge app?**

WSP – Product Badge is our top recommendation for merchants looking for automated product badges, custom labels, and scheduled promotions without editing theme code.

### **Are there free Shopify product badge apps?**

Yes. WSP – Product Badge is free, while several other apps, including Hoppy and Rimix, also offer free options. Always check their current Shopify App Store pricing.

### **Can Shopify product badges increase sales?**

Product badges can improve product visibility and communicate promotions more clearly. Their impact on sales depends on badge placement, design, product relevance, and the overall shopping experience.

### **Do product badge apps require coding?**

Most modern Shopify product badge apps offer no-code installation and customization. However, available features and theme compatibility vary between apps.
